    How the Satellite Explorer Works
    1. Geocoding

    Uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim (free, no key) to resolve place names to coordinates. Results are shown as map pins.

    2. Satellite Links

    Deep-links to Sentinel Hub EO Browser, NASA Earthdata Worldview, and Copernicus Data Space are generated from your coordinates. No imagery is downloaded automatically.

    3. Image Analysis

    Your uploaded image is analysed with OpenCV + NumPy. Four pixel-level indicators (colour anomaly, turbidity, algae bloom, dark patches) are scored 0–100 and combined into a composite risk score.

    4. Visual Overlay

    Detected suspicious regions are highlighted with colour-coded bounding boxes and semi-transparent fills. A legend is embedded in the image. Download the annotated image for offline reports.

    API & Service Notes: Nominatim geocoding is limited to ~1 request/second (OSM policy). Sentinel Hub EO Browser and Copernicus Data Space require a free account to view imagery. NASA Worldview requires no sign-in. Google Maps satellite view is always accessible. None of these services' imagery is fetched or embedded here — all links open in your browser. Visual analysis results are experimental estimates only and never constitute proof of chemical pollution.

    Analysis Methodology (Transparent)
    Colour Anomaly

    Detects hues outside the healthy blue/blue-green water palette. Brownish, yellowish, or reddish tones may indicate turbidity or runoff.

    Turbidity Proxy

    Uses brightness variance (L* channel) as a proxy for suspended particles, sediment, or foam that scatter light.

    Algae Bloom

    Green-channel dominance over blue is a visual marker of algae/cyanobacteria blooms driven by nutrient overload.

    Dark Patch Detection

    Very dark, low-saturation regions can signal oil sheens, heavy organic matter, or sedimentation on the water surface.
